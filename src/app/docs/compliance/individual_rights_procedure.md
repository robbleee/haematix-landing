# Individual Rights

**Last updated:** 7 July 2026  
**Version:** 2.0

Individuals may have rights under UK GDPR in relation to personal data processed by Haemio Ltd.

## Your Rights

Depending on the context, you may have the right to:

- access your personal data;
- correct inaccurate personal data;
- request deletion;
- restrict processing;
- object to processing;
- receive certain data in a portable format;
- complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

## How to Make a Request

Email [robert.lee@haem.io](mailto:robert.lee@haem.io) with:

- your name;
- the organisation you are connected with, if relevant;
- the right you want to exercise;
- enough information for us to identify the relevant data;
- proof of identity if requested.

Please do not include identifiable patient information in ordinary email unless necessary, authorised, and sent through an appropriate secure channel.

## Customer Deployments

If your request relates to a customer deployment where a hospital, clinic, research group, or other customer is controller, we may need to refer the request to that customer.

Where Haemio Ltd acts as processor, we will assist the customer controller according to the customer agreement and DPA.

## Response Time

We aim to respond within one month. If a request is complex or numerous, we may extend the response period where allowed by law and will explain why.

## ICO

You can contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office at [https://ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk). We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concern first.
