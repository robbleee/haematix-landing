# Legal Basis Overview

**Last updated:** 7 July 2026  
**Version:** 2.0

This overview explains the typical legal bases Haemio Ltd expects to rely on. The correct basis for a specific deployment depends on the customer, purpose, data, and clinical/research context.

## Website and Business Data

| Processing | Typical Legal Basis |
| --- | --- |
| Responding to enquiries | legitimate interests; steps before a contract |
| Customer and supplier administration | contract; legitimate interests |
| Security logs and abuse prevention | legitimate interests; legal obligation where applicable |
| Website analytics | legitimate interests; consent where required |
| Contract and legal recordkeeping | legal obligation; legitimate interests |

## Hosted Customer Deployments

Where Haemio Ltd hosts or operates a customer deployment, the customer usually determines the lawful basis as controller. Haemio Ltd usually processes the data as processor under the customer agreement and DPA.

For health, genetic, pathology, and clinical data, the customer must identify:

- an Article 6 lawful basis under UK GDPR; and
- an Article 9 condition for special category data.

Depending on the deployment, these may relate to healthcare provision, healthcare management, public interest in health, research, or another valid legal basis. Haemio Ltd does not decide this for the customer unless Haemio Ltd is acting as controller for a specific processing activity.

## On-Premise Deployments

For on-premise deployments operated by the customer without Haemio Ltd access to live data, the customer controls the processing environment and legal basis. Haemio Ltd may not process patient data unless it receives access for support, maintenance, managed services, or another documented purpose.

## Public Website Tools

The public website is not intended for identifiable patient data. Users should not submit patient names, NHS numbers, dates of birth, addresses, or other direct identifiers.
